## Key takeaways

1. **No deep-learning SDF paper on TSE exists** — closest are classical SDF tests (Taleblou et al.: behavioral SDF, sentiment SDF, beta-vs-SDF efficiency; Dolou & Badri: Fama-MacBeth + SDF idiosyncratic risk) — all use classical econometrics, none use neural networks. The CPZ replication fills this gap.
2. TSE ML/DL literature is dominated by **return/price prediction** (LSTM, CNN-LSTM, GRU, wavelet+NN, fuzzy NN, ANN vs multi-factor comparisons) — not pricing/valuation.
3. Persian journals publish in Shamsi years; citation needs year conversion (1403 SH ≈ 2024-25 Gregorian).
